Scan your recharge voucher and top up in seconds.

Install QuikFlex Open QuikFlex
Scan Point your camera at your voucher.
Detect QuikFlex reads the voucher automatically.
Top Up Confirm and let your phone handle the rest.

Install on iPhone

QuikFlex works as a web app directly from Safari.

  1. Open QuikFlex in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap "Add".

Install on Android

QuikFlex can be installed from a supported browser.

  1. Open QuikFlex in Chrome.
  2. Tap the browser menu.
  3. Choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Confirm the installation.

Once installed, QuikFlex can scan vouchers offline.
